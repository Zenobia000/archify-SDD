# CareSlot reference document pack

CareSlot is a **fictional** clinic appointment and reminder platform. It exists
only to demonstrate how Archify diagrams form a set of design documents
(BRD, SRS, SAD, SDD, ADR) with traceable relations between abstraction levels.
Nothing here describes a real product or organisation.

- `careslot.pack.json` groups the diagrams into documents and declares shared elements.
- `sources/` holds the authored Archify JSON (and one explainer for the ADR).
- `artifacts/` is generated by `npm --prefix archify run build:packs`; do not edit it by hand.

## Domain facts (single source for every diagram)

Actors: **Patient**, **Front-desk staff**, **Doctor**, **Clinic admin**.

External systems: **Identity Provider** (OIDC sign-in), **Payment Provider**
(card deposits and refunds), **Messaging Provider** (SMS and LINE delivery),
**Clinic EMR** (existing electronic medical record; receives confirmed visits).

Business rules:

1. A patient books an open slot; the slot is held for 10 minutes.
2. A deposit is taken to confirm; the appointment becomes `confirmed` only after payment succeeds.
3. Reminders go out 24 h and 2 h before the visit; the patient may reschedule until 2 h before.
4. A no-show forfeits the deposit; a cancellation more than 24 h before is refunded.
5. Confirmed visits are pushed to the Clinic EMR.

### Data entities (ERD tables)

`patients`, `doctors`, `slots`, `appointments`, `payments`, `reminders`, `outbox_events`.
Element `D-10` = Appointment, `D-11` = Slot, `D-12` = Payment.

### Business processes

`OP-10` Book a visit · `OP-20` Pay deposit · `OP-30` Send reminders · `OP-40` Reschedule or cancel.

### Appointment states

`held` → `confirmed` → `reminded` → `checked_in` → `completed`; also `expired`
(hold timeout), `cancelled`, `no_show`.
